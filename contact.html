What happens next

After you
hit send.

We try to make the first few steps as simple and pressure-free as possible. There is no obligation, no chasing salesperson, and nothing in writing until you're comfortable proceeding.

STEP 01

We reply within 1 day

An email from Sam acknowledging your enquiry, with two or three time slots for an initial call. No automated responses, no marketing chains.

STEP 02

60-minute call

A confidential conversation to understand your hotel, your goals and your constraints. We'll be honest about whether we're a good fit.

STEP 03

NDA & site visit

If we're proceeding, NDA in place and we visit the property. Light-touch — we don't need to disrupt operations or alert staff at this stage.

STEP 04

Written proposal

Indicative numbers, structure and terms in writing. You decide whether to proceed. If not, no fee, no follow-up — your information stays confidential.
