What happens next

The first
conversation.

We've intentionally kept the early stages light. There's no pitch deck, no big team on a Zoom call. Just an honest exchange about whether the structures we offer might fit what you're trying to achieve.

DAY 01

You send a message

Even a paragraph is enough. We'll respond within one working day acknowledging and proposing a call time.

WEEK 01

60-minute discovery call

Hosted by group leadership. We talk through your situation, your goals, the property, and which structures might fit.

WEEK 02–04

Indicative proposal

If we both want to take it further, we share an NDA, review your trading history, and produce a written proposal with indicative numbers.

WEEK 04+

Heads of terms

If the proposal lands, we agree heads of terms, instruct legals, and move to formal documentation. Operational handover typically follows 30–60 days later.
