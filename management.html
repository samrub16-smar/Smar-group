Hotel Management,
done right.

A full-service management agreement is the simplest way to step back from operations without giving up ownership. You keep the freehold, the asset value, and the upside. We take everything else.

Why management

Keep the asset.
Lose the headache.

A hotel management agreement is the most flexible structure available to a UK hotel owner. Ownership stays with you. Daily operations move to us. The hotel continues to trade under its existing brand, with continuity of staff (TUPE'd into Smar Group), and you receive both income and detailed monthly reporting.

BENEFIT 01

You retain ownership

The freehold, leasehold or company shares remain with you. We operate under a management contract — typically 5 to 15 years with break clauses.

BENEFIT 02

You keep the upside

All trading profit flows to you after operating costs and our management fee. As the business grows, you grow with it. We're incentivised to drive performance.

BENEFIT 03

Operational continuity

Existing staff transfer to us under TUPE. Suppliers retained where appropriate. Guests notice nothing on day one — except service quality starts going up.

BENEFIT 04

Cost efficiency

Cross-trained staff, shared procurement across our portfolio, and SMR FM's London-scale buying power deliver payroll and supply-cost reductions from month one.

BENEFIT 05

Transparent reporting

Monthly P&L pack. Live KPI dashboard. Quarterly strategy review. You always know what's happening in your hotel — without having to be there.

BENEFIT 06

Clean exit option

Convert to a lease later, sell with an in-place operator, or take operations back at the end of the term. The structure is yours to evolve.

Commercial structure

How the deal works.

Every management agreement is bespoke, but the framework is consistent. Below is the typical shape of a Smar Group management contract. Final terms depend on the property, trading history, and your priorities.

Standard Management

Base fee + incentive.

The most common structure. We charge a base management fee as a percentage of gross revenue, plus an incentive fee tied to GOP performance — so we're paid more when you earn more.

  • Base fee: typically 3–5% of gross revenue
  • Incentive fee: 8–12% of GOP above an agreed threshold
  • Term: 5–15 years with mutual break clauses
  • Owner approval required for capex above set thresholds
  • Quarterly strategy reviews with hotel owner

Performance Management

Lower base, higher upside.

For owners who want to align fully with performance. We take a smaller base fee (or sometimes none) in exchange for a larger share of GOP improvement above an agreed baseline.

  • Reduced or waived base fee
  • Higher incentive share — typically 18–25% above baseline GOP
  • Baseline agreed from trailing 24 months trading
  • Best fit for under-performing or transitional assets
  • Term: typically 7–10 years

Scope of management

What sits with us.

When we say "full service", we mean it. Below is the complete operational scope that transfers to Smar Group under a standard management agreement.

01

People & Payroll

All staff TUPE'd into Smar Group. Recruitment, payroll, training, performance management, employment law compliance.

02

Daily Operations

Revenue Management

Rate strategy, OTA channel management, dynamic pricing, corporate accounts and group sales.

Your side of the table

What stays
with you.

A management agreement isn't a sale. You retain ownership, you retain control of the asset's strategic direction, and you retain the upside. Smar Group operates; you own. Here's exactly what stays in your hands.

RETAINED 01

Freehold & asset value

All property rights remain with you. As the hotel's underlying value grows, you capture every penny of capital appreciation.

RETAINED 02

Trading profit

All operating profit flows to you after costs and our agreed fees. The better the hotel trades, the more you earn.

RETAINED 03

Strategic direction

Major capex, brand decisions, repositioning strategy and disposal timing — your call. We advise; you decide.

RETAINED 04

Sale optionality

Sell the hotel any time. The management contract becomes part of the offer — often making the asset more attractive to institutional buyers.

RETAINED 05

Visibility & oversight

Quarterly review meetings, live dashboard access, owner approval rights over budget and major decisions. Eyes on, hands off.

RETAINED 06

Termination rights

Performance-linked break clauses give you exit rights if benchmarks aren't met. The relationship works because both sides earn it.
