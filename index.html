Who we are

Operators first.
Not consultants.

Smar Group is uniquely placed to understand the demands of running a hotel — because it's our daily business. We operate properties across the UK ranging from heritage Scottish country houses to newly-branded Spark by Hilton properties, supported by our SMR Facilities Management arm that serves over fifty hospitality clients across London.

That dual identity is what sets us apart. We are not a consultancy advising from a distance. We are not a chain bolting management contracts onto a pricing model. We are a working operator with the systems, people and reporting infrastructure to take a hotel's operational weight off its owner.

Choose full management and retain ownership. Choose a long-term lease and step out of operations entirely. Or build a hybrid arrangement that fits your timeline. The relationship is shaped around you.

Two paths forward

How would
you like to
step back?

Whether you want to retain ownership and outsource operations, or sell the operating responsibility entirely in exchange for a guaranteed monthly cheque — we structure the relationship around what works for you. Most owner relationships start with a single conversation about which path fits.

Option 01 — Management

We run it.
You own it.

Smar Group becomes your operator. We handle every aspect of running the hotel — staffing, housekeeping, F&B, maintenance, distribution, compliance — and report to you on performance. You keep ownership and the upside.

  • Full operational management for a defined management fee
  • Performance-linked incentive structure available
  • Existing brand affiliations retained where valuable
  • Monthly P&L and live KPI dashboard access via SmarCEO
  • TUPE-compliant staff transition handled in-house
How management works →
Option 02 — Lease

Fixed return.
Full exit.

We take a long-term lease on your property and pay you a fixed rent every month. You exit operations entirely — no staffing headaches, no seasonal worry. Pure passive income against a real asset you still own.

  • Guaranteed monthly rent — typically index-linked
  • Long-term lease structures (10–25 years) with break options
  • Smar Group assumes all operational risk and capex obligation
  • Retain freehold ownership and long-term asset appreciation
  • Ideal for retiring operators or generational succession plans

How it works

From first call
to handover
in 4 steps.

Whether you're considering management or lease, the diligence path looks similar. We move at the pace owners need — from initial confidential conversation to full operational handover, typically inside 60–90 days.

STEP 01

Confidential call

A 60-minute call to understand your situation, your goals, and which structure (management, lease or hybrid) makes sense.

STEP 02

Property review

We review trading accounts, contracts, staff, and walk the property under NDA. Output: a written proposal with indicative numbers.

STEP 03

Heads of terms

We agree commercial terms, sign heads, run legal due diligence and finalise the management or lease agreement.

STEP 04

Operational handover

TUPE process, system migration, staff briefing, supplier transition. You step back, we step in — typically in a single weekend.
