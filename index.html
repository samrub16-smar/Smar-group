Two paths forward

How would you
like to step back?

Whether you want to retain ownership and outsource operations, or sell the operating responsibility entirely in exchange for a guaranteed monthly cheque — we structure the relationship around what works for you. Most of our owner relationships start with a single conversation about which path fits.

Option 01 — Management

We run it.
You own it.

Smar Group becomes your operator. We handle every aspect of running the hotel — staffing, housekeeping, F&B, maintenance, distribution, compliance — and report to you on performance. You keep the upside.

  • Full operational management for a defined management fee
  • Performance-linked incentive structure available
  • Existing brand affiliations (Best Western, independent) retained
  • You receive transparent monthly P&L and KPI reporting
  • TUPE-compliant staff transition handled by our HR team
See how management works →
Option 02 — Lease

Fixed return.
Zero involvement.

We take a long-term lease on your property and pay you a fixed rent every month. You exit operations entirely — no staffing headaches, no seasonal worry, no midnight calls about a broken boiler. Pure passive income against a real asset.

  • Guaranteed monthly rent — typically index-linked
  • Long-term lease structures (10–25 years) with break options
  • Smar Group assumes all operational risk and capex obligation
  • Retain freehold ownership and long-term asset appreciation
  • Ideal for retiring operators or generational succession plans
See lease structures →
Not sure which? There's a third path. Hybrid arrangements: start with management, transition to lease, or build a custom structure around a planned exit.

Scope of work

Everything between
check-in and check-out.

When Smar Group takes on a hotel, we take on everything that makes it run. You retain ownership and strategic oversight — we own the day-to-day. Below is the working scope of a typical management agreement.

01

Front of House

Reception, reservations, concierge, guest experience, complaint resolution and night management — all staffed, trained and led by our team.

02

Housekeeping

Daily room servicing, deep-clean rotations, linen management, public area cleanliness and quality auditing to brand standard.

03

F&B Operations

Restaurant, bar, breakfast service, weddings and events — kitchen brigade, FOH service, menu engineering and supplier management.

04

Maintenance & Engineering

Planned preventative maintenance, reactive repairs, statutory compliance (fire, gas, water hygiene) and capex project management.

05

Sales & Distribution

OTA channel management, group and corporate sales, rate strategy, brand standards compliance for Best Western and other affiliations.

06

HR & Compliance

Recruitment, training, payroll, TUPE transitions, employment law, right-to-work, GDPR and full hospitality compliance.

How it works

From first call
to handover
in 4 steps.

Whether you're considering management or lease, the diligence path looks similar. We move at the pace owners need — from an initial confidential conversation to full operational handover typically inside 60–90 days.

STEP 01

Confidential conversation

A 60-minute call to understand your situation, your goals, and which structure (management, lease or hybrid) makes sense.

STEP 02

Property & financial review

We review trading accounts, contracts, staff, and walk the property under NDA. Output: a written proposal with indicative numbers.

STEP 03

Heads of terms & legals

We agree commercial terms, sign heads, run legal due diligence and finalise the management or lease agreement.

STEP 04

Operational handover

TUPE process, system migration, staff briefing, supplier transition. You step back, we step in — typically in a single weekend.
