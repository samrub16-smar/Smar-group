Smar Group  /  Lease & Exit

Fixed return.
Full exit.

For owners ready to step away from the hotel business but keep the asset. Lease your property to Smar Group, receive guaranteed monthly rent, and convert an operating business into a long-term income stream.

Who this is for

If any of these sound
like you...

A lease is the cleanest way to exit hotel operations without selling the asset. We've structured leases for owners in all of the following situations — if your circumstance sounds similar, we should talk.

SCENARIO 01

Retirement

You've spent decades running the hotel. You want income from the asset but not the 6 a.m. phone calls. A lease lets you walk away cleanly.

SCENARIO 02

Generational handover

Children don't want to take on the operation. Family wants to retain the property as a long-term holding. A lease converts it to passive income for the next generation.

SCENARIO 03

Burnout

The business consumed years of your life. You're done. You want out — but selling outright feels premature given asset values. Lease bridges the gap.

SCENARIO 04

Asset diversification

You've built a property portfolio and the hotel is the operational outlier. Convert it to a rental property and treat it like the rest of your holdings.

SCENARIO 05

Health or life changes

Circumstances change. You need to disengage operationally without forcing a fire-sale of the asset. A lease gives you time and income.

SCENARIO 06

Pre-sale optimisation

Selling in 5–10 years? An in-place lease with a credible operator makes the asset more saleable to institutional buyers — and reduces your operational risk in the meantime.

Structure

How the
lease works.

Smar Group becomes the tenant. We take over the operating business — staff, contracts, licences, brand affiliations — and pay you a fixed rent every month for the duration of the lease. You retain freehold ownership of the property itself.

Key terms

Every lease is bespoke and depends on the property, trading history, and your objectives. The structure below is typical — final terms agreed in heads.

  • Term: 10–25 years with mutual break options
  • Rent: fixed monthly, typically index-linked (RPI or CPI)
  • Reviews: open-market or stepped reviews at 5-year intervals
  • Repair obligations: typically full repairing and insuring (FRI)
  • Capex: tenant responsible for operational capex; landlord for structural
  • Guarantees: rent guarantees or deposits agreed in heads

The handover

What moves to
Smar Group.

On completion of the lease, the entire operating business transfers to Smar Group. You retain the property; we retain everything that runs on it. The handover is typically completed inside 60 days from heads of terms.

Transfers to Smar Group

Everything operational.

  • All employment contracts (TUPE compliant)
  • All supplier and trade contracts
  • Booking system, PMS data and forward reservations
  • Operating licences (alcohol, food, entertainment)
  • Brand affiliations (Best Western, etc.)
  • F&B stock, OS&E, consumables
  • All operational P&L responsibility
  • Capex obligation (per lease terms)
Retained by you

The asset itself.

  • Freehold or long leasehold ownership
  • All capital appreciation of the property
  • Structural repair obligation (per lease terms)
  • Right to sell the property (subject to lease)
  • Building insurance (recovered via lease)
  • Mortgage relationships and refinancing rights
  • Long-term strategic optionality on the asset
  • Monthly rental income for the lease term

A third option

Hybrid &
phased exits.

Many owners don't want a binary choice. We've structured arrangements that start as management agreements and convert to lease at a later trigger date — giving you time, optionality, and a planned glide path out of operations.

Manage-then-lease

Bridge structure.

Start with a management agreement for 2–3 years. We prove performance, stabilise the asset, and convert to a lease at an agreed rent based on stabilised trading.

  • Ideal for owners who want to "test the relationship"
  • Lease rent calibrated to demonstrated performance
  • Smooth transition for staff and brand
  • Reduces information asymmetry on lease pricing
Phased exit

Custom structure.

Built around your timeline. Whether you want to exit in stages, transition over a set period, or align with a planned property sale — we'll build the structure backwards from your goal.

  • Aligned with retirement or succession timing
  • Can include consultancy retainer to retain your expertise
  • Capex programme phased over the transition
  • Tax-efficient structures explored with your advisors
