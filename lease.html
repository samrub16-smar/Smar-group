Fixed return.
Full exit.

For owners ready to step out of operations entirely. We take a long-term lease on your hotel, you receive a guaranteed monthly rent, and you keep the freehold — along with all long-term capital appreciation of the underlying asset.

Who it's for

When a lease
fits best.

A lease is the right structure when you want certainty and a clean operational exit. The trade-off compared to management is simple: you give up some upside in exchange for predictable monthly income and zero operational risk. These are the owner situations where leases tend to win.

SCENARIO 01

Retiring operators

You've run the hotel for years. You want to step back fully — keep the asset, draw a monthly income, and stop being on call. A lease delivers exactly that.

SCENARIO 02

Generational succession

The next generation doesn't want to run the business. Convert operations to a lease, retain the family asset, and pass a passive income stream through the family company.

SCENARIO 03

Bank-backed owners

Your lender wants predictable debt service coverage. A long-term, index-linked lease is the cleanest income story you can show them.

SCENARIO 04

Portfolio rationalisation

You own multiple properties and want to convert one or two into pure passive holdings, while keeping the operational ones in-house or under management.

SCENARIO 05

Capex-heavy assets

The hotel needs investment you don't want to fund. Under a lease, all operational capex moves to us — agreed up front in the lease schedule.

SCENARIO 06

Investor owners

You bought the hotel as an asset, not as an operation. A lease gives you the income stream you wanted in the first place, without the operational learning curve.

How the lease works

Standard
lease
structure.

We use a standard institutional lease framework. Final terms are bespoke to each property — but the framework below is consistent. We can also build hybrid structures (management today, lease later) if that better suits the timing.

The Lease Framework

What's in the contract.

The lease is institutional in form — built around the same principles a pension fund or REIT would use. Indexed rent, long term, full repairing and insuring, with break and rent review at agreed intervals.

  • Term: typically 10 to 25 years
  • Monthly rent paid in advance, by standing order
  • Rent reviews every 5 years — typically RPI or CPI linked, with cap/collar
  • FRI (full repairing & insuring) — we cover all operational capex
  • Mutual break options at year 10 (or as negotiated)
  • Owner retains freehold and all asset appreciation

Hybrid & phased options

Not either / or.

Most owner conversations don't end with a binary decision. They end with a structure shaped to your timeline — management for the first few years while you stay close to the business, converting to a lease at an agreed trigger point. Or a lease from day one with a profit-share top-up if trading exceeds an agreed baseline. We design the relationship around how you want to wind down — not the other way round.

Process

From first call
to first
cheque.

Lease conversations move a bit more slowly than management ones — there's more legal and financial diligence involved, and you'll typically want your own advisors present. Realistic timeline from first call to rent commencing is 90–120 days.

STEP 01

Initial conversation

A confidential call to understand the property, your goals and your timeline. Output: indicative rent range based on a high-level review.

STEP 02

Property diligence

Trading review, building condition survey, capex audit. We provide a written lease term sheet with definite rent, term and review structure.

STEP 03

Lease negotiation

Heads of terms signed. Lawyers engaged on both sides. Final lease drafted, reviewed and executed. Standard property lawyer scope.

STEP 04

Handover & first rent

TUPE staff transition, supplier transition, system migration over a single weekend. First rent paid on commencement date.
