What we cover

The operational
backbone.

SMR Facilities Management delivers the full operational stack for hotels — mix and match the services you need. We're built around the hospitality industry's actual rhythms: weekend peaks, seasonal swings, sudden cover requirements, and the relentless need for consistency.

SERVICE 01

Housekeeping

Daily room servicing teams, deep-clean rotations, linen management, public area cleanliness. Scaled up and down with your occupancy.

SERVICE 02

Front of House Staffing

Reception, night porters, concierge — permanent placements or flexible cover for holidays, sickness or peak periods.

SERVICE 03

F&B Staffing

Chefs, kitchen porters, waiting staff, bar staff. Event cover for weddings and conferences. Rapid deployment when you need it.

SERVICE 04

Maintenance

On-call maintenance engineers, planned preventative maintenance schedules, statutory compliance (fire, gas, water, electrical).

SERVICE 05

HR & Compliance

Full HR outsourcing — recruitment, contracts, payroll, performance management, right-to-work, training, employment law support.

SERVICE 06

TUPE Process

Hotel changing ownership or operator? We manage the entire TUPE transfer process — consultation, documentation, compliance.

SERVICE 07

Training

Hospitality-specific training across all roles, with formal qualifications where required. Cross-discipline training reduces payroll dependency.

SERVICE 08

Property Care

Grounds maintenance, window cleaning, specialist cleaning, periodic deep cleans. Everything beyond daily housekeeping.

SERVICE 09

Single Point of Contact

One number, one account manager, one team. Stop juggling multiple suppliers — coordinate everything operational through SMR.

The economics

How outsourcing
actually saves money.

Hoteliers often assume an external FM provider must cost more. In practice, the opposite is true. Here's exactly how the maths works in your favour when you consolidate operational services with SMR.

SAVING 01

Cross-trained staff

Our team members are trained across multiple disciplines. A housekeeper can step into F&B service; a maintenance engineer can cover events. Lower headcount, same coverage.

SAVING 02

Variable cost structure

Stop carrying fixed payroll through low season. SMR scales staffing to occupancy — you only pay for the hours your hotel actually needs.

SAVING 03

No agency mark-ups

Direct employment by SMR — not a chain of agencies and sub-agencies. Costs are transparent and consistent.

SAVING 04

Lower staff turnover

A career path across multiple sites and disciplines means our teams stay longer. Less recruitment, less retraining, more consistent quality.

SAVING 05

Consolidated HR overhead

No need for an in-house HR function. Payroll, contracts, compliance, employment disputes — all absorbed into the SMR service.

SAVING 06

Compliance built-in

Right-to-work, training records, statutory compliance — all maintained as part of our service. No fines, no surprises, no separate compliance budget.
