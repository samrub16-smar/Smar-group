Smar Group  /  About

Owner-operators
since day one.

Smar Group is a privately-owned UK hospitality and facilities business. We don't advise from a desk — we run hotels. We've built every system we use from the operational floor up. When you hand us your property, you're partnering with operators, not consultants.

Smar Group started not with a hotel, but with a problem. London hoteliers — clients of our founder for over a decade — kept describing the same frustration: brilliant operational teams, dragged down by fragmented suppliers, agency mark-ups, and the impossibility of finding one partner who could handle the whole operational stack.

That problem became SMR Facilities Management. Over more than ten years, we've grown to support over fifty London-area hotels with housekeeping, staffing, HR and maintenance — built around the actual rhythms of hospitality rather than the spreadsheet logic of generic facilities providers.

Three years ago, we took the logical next step: rather than support hoteliers from the sidelines, become a hotelier. We acquired our first hotel — the Manor in Exmouth — and proved that the SMR operational engine could power a full hotel business. Two more properties followed: Pitbauchlie House and Balgeddie House, both in Fife, Scotland. Today we operate three hotels under the Smar Group umbrella, totalling over 110 bedrooms.

We don't advise from a desk — we run hotels. Every system we sell, we use.

That dual identity — operator and service provider — is what makes our offer different. We're not a chain trying to bolt on management contracts. We're not a consultancy without operational scars. We're a working hotel business that knows what it costs, in money and in attention, to keep a property running well — and we've decided to take that burden off other owners who don't want to carry it anymore.

Group structure

Three companies.
One group.

Smar Group sits over three trading entities, each focused on a specific part of the operation. The structure exists for legal and operational clarity — for owners working with us, it's all one team with one point of accountability.

ENTITY 01

Smar Hotels Ltd

Holding company for our Devon operations including the Manor Hotel Exmouth. Trading entity for Devon-based hotel income, capex and operations.

ENTITY 02

Smar Hotels & Leisure Ltd

Holding company for our Scottish operations including Pitbauchlie House and Balgeddie House. Trades the leisure-led elements including pool, gym and spa facilities at Balgeddie.

ENTITY 03

SMR Facilities Management Ltd

The London-based FM business. Provides staffing, housekeeping, maintenance and HR services to over 50 London hoteliers, and operates as the back-office for the group's three hotels.

How we work

Four principles.
Non-negotiable.

We've taken on hotels and dropped them again because the relationship wasn't right. The four principles below are the lens we apply to every owner conversation — and the standard we hold ourselves to once we're inside a property.

PRINCIPLE 01

Honesty over polish

If the numbers won't work, we'll say so in the first meeting. We don't pitch deals we can't deliver, and we don't promise margins we know aren't there.

PRINCIPLE 02

People first

Hospitality businesses are people businesses. We TUPE staff carefully, retain good operators, train hard, and pay properly. Cutting corners on payroll always costs more downstream.

PRINCIPLE 03

Visible reporting

Owners shouldn't have to chase. Monthly P&L, weekly flash, live dashboard. Bad news arrives early, with the plan to fix it attached.

PRINCIPLE 04

Long-term thinking

A hotel that's stripped for short-term yield doesn't stay valuable. We invest in maintenance, staff and standards — protecting your asset's value as well as its income.
