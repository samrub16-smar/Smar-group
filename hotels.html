From a Regency coaching inn on the South Devon coast to two listed Scottish country houses, our portfolio is proof of operational range. One team, three very different properties, consistent results.

01 / Devon
Exmouth · South Devon

Manor Hotel Exmouth

One of the oldest hostelries in Exmouth. The Manor sits on the Beacon — a Regency crescent overlooking the Exe Estuary — and has welcomed guests since 1790. Twenty-eight en-suite bedrooms, many with direct sea views.

The hotel houses Hat or Lemon, an on-site bistro and cocktail bar, and is five minutes walk from Exmouth Marina, the beach, and the South West Coast Path. Recently refurbished bathrooms; ongoing programme of room upgrades.

02 / Fife
Dunfermline · Fife

Pitbauchlie House Hotel

A Scottish country house hotel operating continuously since 1970. Over 50 bedrooms set in three acres of mature wooded grounds, ten minutes from the Forth bridges and seventeen miles from Edinburgh.

One of central Scotland's leading wedding venues — the Elgin Suite hosts up to 140 guests, with smaller private rooms for intimate celebrations. Harvey's Bistro serves seasonal Scottish menus daily. Sure Hotel Collection by Best Western.

03 / Fife
Glenrothes · Fife

Balgeddie House Hotel

A 1923 listed country house on the outskirts of Glenrothes, set in mature gardens with rolling lawns and a former croquet lawn now used as a summer patio. Thirty-three en-suite bedrooms including the Spencer Nairn and Crombie suites.

Full leisure facilities including a 17-metre indoor pool, sauna and steam room. The Brasserie restaurant serves contemporary Scottish cuisine with views across the grounds. Oak-panelled cocktail bar with an extensive malt whisky list. Best Western affiliated.

Why this matters

Operating proof,
not just promises.

When you hand a hotel to a management company, what matters is whether they can actually run hotels. Our three properties — each different in style, location and scale — are working proof. Every team, every system, every supplier relationship in our toolkit was built running these.

PROOF 01

Geographic range

From South Devon to Central Scotland. We've built operational infrastructure that works regardless of where your hotel is — including remote payroll, regional procurement and on-site management.

PROOF 02

Property variety

Coastal Regency, Scottish country house, leisure-focused listed building. Each requires a different operational rhythm — we've built playbooks for all of them.

PROOF 03

Brand experience

We operate Best Western Sure Hotel Collection properties and independent. We know how to maintain brand standards and how to run successfully outside a brand system.

PROOF 04

Revenue diversity

Pure leisure stays, corporate accommodation, weddings & events, F&B trade. Our hotels earn from all of these — and our owner reporting tracks each channel separately.

PROOF 05

Listed building expertise

Two of our three hotels are listed buildings. We know how to manage planning permissions, conservation officers, and operate within heritage constraints.

PROOF 06

Leisure operations

Balgeddie's pool, sauna and gym add operational complexity many provincial hotels don't have. We've built compliance, safety and maintenance protocols around it.
