01 · South Devon
Exmouth · South Devon · EX8

Manor Hotel Exmouth

A Grade II Regency hotel on Exmouth's Beacon. Built in 1790 as a private residence and converted to a coaching inn in the Victorian era. Twenty-eight sea-view bedrooms, the Hat or Lemon bistro and bar, and one of the finest elevated views over the Exe Estuary on the south coast.

Strong leisure trading from spring through autumn, with growing corporate and wedding business midweek. Operating company: Smar Hotels Ltd.

02 · Fife · Spark by Hilton
Dunfermline · Fife · KY11

Spark by Hilton Dunfermline

A 50-bedroom property set in three acres of mature wooded grounds, ten minutes from the Forth bridges and 30 minutes from Edinburgh Airport. Recently transitioned to the Spark by Hilton brand — operating on Hilton's PEP (Property Engagement Platform) PMS.

Home to Harvey's Bistro and the Elgin Suite, a premier wedding venue in Fife for up to 140 guests. Strong corporate midweek trading from the Dunfermline and Edinburgh business community.

03 · Fife · Listed
Glenrothes · Fife · KY6

Balgeddie House Hotel

A 1923 listed country house on the outskirts of Glenrothes, set in mature grounds. Thirty-three bedrooms, a brasserie restaurant, oak-panelled whisky bar with arched mirrors and chandelier, and a 17-metre indoor swimming pool.

Forty minutes from St Andrews — popular as a golf-tour base — with strong corporate, leisure and wedding trading. The combination of listed-building heritage and full leisure facility is rare in the local market.

Weddings & Events

A meaningful

Weddings and events are not an afterthought in our properties — they're a deliberate commercial strategy. The Elgin Suite at our Fife property is one of the area's go-to venues, hosting civil ceremonies, receptions and corporate events year-round.

Our events teams handle package design, supplier coordination, bespoke menu development and day-of delivery — with volume consistency that keeps F&B margins healthy without compromising the guest experience.
